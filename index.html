Best personal projects

Cable-driven lunar crane

Feb 2026 – Aug 2026 · Research internship, ESA Spaceship Poland

Full-scale cable-driven crane that autonomously unloads cargo from the top of a lunar lander. I designed the mechanical structure, a vision system for cargo position estimation, and a model predictive controller (MPC) based on a dynamic model I derived. The whole system runs in real time on a single-board computer.
