Projects

Cable-driven lunar crane

Feb 2026 – present · ESA Spaceship Poland, Bachelor's thesis

A 70 kg cable-driven crane, inspired by NASA's LSMS, that autonomously unloads cargo from the top of a lunar lander. I designed and built the mechanical structure and wrote the whole software stack: the dynamic model, a nonlinear MPC in acados with an EKF running in real time on a Raspberry Pi 5 (ros2_control, 50 Hz), a cascaded control structure that reduced the tracking error from 3 cm to 0.9 cm, a MuJoCo digital twin calibrated on hardware, input shaping of the cable mode, and a vision pipeline (RealSense, YOLO, RANSAC) for autonomous pick-and-place.

code

FOC motor driver

Oct 2026 – present · Magility, personal project

A round 24 V field-oriented control driver for the Eaglepower LA8308 motor (STM32G431, DRV8323, phase-shunt current sensing, 14-bit magnetic encoder, CAN FD). I designed the schematic and PCB and wrote bare-metal C firmware: FOC at 20 kHz with a current loop tuned from motor parameters identified on hardware, PLL speed estimation, an impedance (MIT-style) mode, protections and PC unit tests. Built and tested on hardware.

code

Quasi-direct-drive actuator

Oct 2026 – Nov 2026 · Magility, personal project

A backdrivable joint module for full-scale legged robots: the LA8308 motor, a 3D-printed nylon 6:1 planetary gearbox, my FOC driver and a CAN FD–USB bridge board, with 16 Nm design peak torque at 700 g. A plug-and-play building block for my future legged robots.

Biped – Physical AI Hackathon, Zurich

Oct 2026 · hackathon

A 6-DoF servo-driven biped built from scratch during the hackathon. It walks with a residual PPO policy trained in MuJoCo that corrects a sine gait. To cross the sim-to-real gap I measured the servos' latency, deadband and lag, modelled them in the simulator, and wrote a C++ ros2_control driver for the servo buses.
